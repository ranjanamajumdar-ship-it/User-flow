If a step needs a different skill type than the assignee has, the system auto-routes that one step. The rest of the flow stays with the primary.

📋
Step types use a 4-bucket workflow model — like your previous app. Every step on the canvas belongs to one of: TO DO (gates & waits) · IN PROGRESS (operator does something) · DONE (terminal outcomes) · ACTIONS (Process · Communication · Validate). 23 step types total in the palette.
