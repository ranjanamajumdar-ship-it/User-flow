Build the flow

Drag step types in from the left, hover between cards to insert, tap a card to edit it.

✨
Drop in Steps (operator does the work) — completion triggers a Card (passive summary with one CTA). Every card’s CTA must route to a different step than the one that opened it.
📋
Step taxonomy — 3 step categories + 1 card category: NORMAL (just do the thing) · BRANCH (choice or shortcut) · EXCEPTION (something went sideways) · CARDS (passive summary, shown after a step, one CTA). Steps are verbs; cards are receipts with one button.
